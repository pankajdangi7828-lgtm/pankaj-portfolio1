About Me

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal. My research bridges the gap between traditional field hydrology and cutting-edge satellite remote sensing.

Primary Research Focus

Fluvial Processes & Modelling

Deep expertise in fluvial processes and fluvial geomorphology. I utilize advanced hydrodynamic and hydrological modelling to understand complex river systems, sediment dynamics, and channel evolution.

Satellite Hydrology & SWOT

Pioneering the application of satellite data in hydrology. My current core work involves the groundbreaking SWOT (Surface Water and Ocean Topography) satellite mission, specifically focusing on validating its data across the massive Ganga and Narmada river basins.
